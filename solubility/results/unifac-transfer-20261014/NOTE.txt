Preliminary restricted subset, not the final assessment.

omits neutral nitro compounds and other charge-separated resonance structures.
The retained comparisons are valid, but coverage is incomplete.
The maintained script now filters on total molecular charge and single-component
identity, with explicit numerical checks for nitromethane, ions, and salts.
