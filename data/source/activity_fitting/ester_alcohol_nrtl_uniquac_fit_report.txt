NOTES
-----

1. The 15 S-series papers are the experimental data sources represented in the
   compiled regression dataset.
2. Pure-component property correlations (Psat, critical properties, UNIQUAC r/q)
   were supplied separately by the user and are therefore not assigned literature
   references in this bibliography.
3. The ethyl acetate + water recommended interaction parameters are intentionally
   retained from the Hong/DECHEMA temperature-dependent treatment rather than
   regressed only to Hong's nine VLLE points.
4. The bibliography records the source paper even when only the relevant binary
   subset of a larger ternary/quaternary study was used.
